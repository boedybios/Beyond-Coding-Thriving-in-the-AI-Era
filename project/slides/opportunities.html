(~1.5 min) These ideas make good portfolio projects because they have real users. A study tool: generate quizzes from your notes; you’ll learn the material and the tech at the same time. A local UMKM: many small businesses near campus still manage orders through chat and notebooks; a simple tool can genuinely help. A campus helper: student organizations always have repetitive admin work. Hackathons: great for learning teamwork under time pressure. Use AI to move faster, for example to draft the first UI, the database schema, or tests, but you make the product decisions.
