03 · Growing in the AI era

Build a portfolio that shows how you think

Real projects

Solve a real problem for real users: your campus, a local business, or your community.

Teamwork

Contribute to open source, team projects, or hackathons. Show you can work with others.

Your process

Document what you tried, what failed, and why you chose your approach.

When anyone can generate code, your reasoning is what stands out.

(~2 min) In the AI era, a working app is easier to produce, so on its own it says less about you. What stands out is evidence of judgment. Real projects: a tutorial clone says little; a small app used by 20 people in your organization or a nearby business says a lot. Teamwork: open source (start with documentation fixes or issues labeled “good first issue”), group projects, and hackathons show you can work with others. Your process: keep a short decision log in your repository. Interviewers often ask “walk me through a decision you made”, and this prepares your answer.
