01 · What’s changing

Writing code is one step out of four

Step 1

Understand

Define the problem and who it’s for.

You lead

Step 2

Design

Choose the approach and trade-offs.

You lead

Step 3

Build

Write the code, tests, and docs.

AI-assisted

Step 4

Evaluate

Check it’s correct, safe, and useful.

You lead

AI can help at every step, but steps 1, 2, and 4 need your judgment. Your role now: design, evaluate, and build with AI.

(~3 min) In class, most effort goes into step 3: writing code. In industry, much of the value comes from steps 1, 2, and 4, and with AI drafting more of step 3, the balance shifts even further. Step 1, Understand: talk to users, clarify requirements, and agree on what “done” means. Step 2, Design: choose the architecture, data model, and libraries, and weigh trade-offs like speed versus simplicity. Step 3, Build: AI can draft code, tests, and docs; you steer and edit. Step 4, Evaluate: test, review, and check whether it really solves the original problem. Then loop back to step 1. So the developer’s role becomes designer, evaluator, and builder who works with AI. Checkpoint: you should be at about minute 13.
