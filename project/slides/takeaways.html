Wrapping up

Five things to remember

1

Coding is one step of four. Understanding, designing, and evaluating matter more than ever.

2

Treat AI as a fast teammate. Give it clear context, then verify what it gives you.

3

Never submit code you can’t explain. Read, run, check, and own it.

4

Invest in fundamentals and people skills. They make your use of AI valuable.

5

Show your thinking, and keep learning. Build things that help real people.
