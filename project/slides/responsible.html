02 · Working with AI

Use AI responsibly: four things to protect

Privacy

Don’t paste personal data or confidential code into public AI tools.

Security

Never share passwords, API keys, or tokens. Review AI code for vulnerabilities.

Fairness

Ask who could be harmed, excluded, or treated unfairly by what you build.

Rights and integrity

Respect licenses and your campus rules. Disclose AI use when it’s required.

When unsure, don’t paste it. Check your campus or company AI policy first.

(~2 min) Four things to protect. Privacy: many public AI tools may store what you paste. Treat the chat box as public unless you know the tool’s policy. Never paste classmates’ or customers’ personal data. In Indonesia, the Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP, Law No. 27 of 2022) also governs how personal data is handled. Security: API keys pasted into prompts or committed to GitHub get leaked. Use environment variables, and review AI code for issues like SQL injection. Fairness: if your app ranks, filters, or makes decisions about people, such as scholarships, hiring, or loans, check who could be treated unfairly. Rights and integrity: AI output can resemble licensed code, so respect licenses. For coursework, follow your lecturer’s rules on AI and be open about how you used it.
