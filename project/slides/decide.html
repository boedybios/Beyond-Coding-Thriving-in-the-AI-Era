(~2 min) A simple way to decide how much to rely on AI. Two questions: how costly is a mistake (the stakes), and how easy is it to check the result? Low stakes, easy to check: let AI draft freely, like formatting, sample data, or boilerplate. You’ll spot errors quickly. Low stakes, hard to check: use AI for ideas, not final answers, like brainstorming names or early research. High stakes, easy to check: AI can draft, but verify carefully with tests and review, like payment logic or database migrations. High stakes, hard to check: human judgment decides, like security design, architecture, or decisions about people. AI can advise, but a person must be accountable. Checkpoint: you should be at about minute 28.
