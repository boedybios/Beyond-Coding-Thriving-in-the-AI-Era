A question to start

If AI can write code,
what is a developer for?

Keep your answer in mind. We’ll come back to it at the end.

(~2 min) Ask the question directly, then pause. Invite two or three quick answers from the room. Typical answers: “to check what AI writes”, “to solve problems”, or even “nothing!”. Don’t settle it yet. Tell them each part of the talk adds a piece of the answer, and you’ll come back to it on the last slide. Optional quick poll: “Raise your hand if you used an AI tool for coding or studying this week.” Usually most hands go up, which shows AI is already part of their daily work.
