03 · Growing in the AI era

Skills that matter: three layers

People

Communication · Collaboration · Understanding users

Turns good code into something people actually use.

Thinking

Problem solving · Critical thinking · Creativity

Helps you frame the right problem and judge AI’s answers.

Foundations

Programming · Algorithms & data structures · How systems work

Lets you understand, debug, and review what AI writes.

Holding it all together: learning how to learn, so you can adapt as tools change.

(~2 min) Read this from the bottom up, like a building. Foundations: programming, algorithms and data structures, and how systems work, such as operating systems, networks, and databases. This is what lets you judge what AI produces. Thinking: problem solving, critical thinking, and creativity. This is framing the right problem and questioning answers, including AI’s. People: communication, collaboration, and understanding users. Many project problems come from misunderstood requirements, not from syntax errors. Holding it together: learning how to learn. Some of the tools you’ll use in your first job may not exist yet today.
