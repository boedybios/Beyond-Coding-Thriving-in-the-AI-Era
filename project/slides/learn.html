03 · Growing in the AI era

Use AI to learn, not to skip learning

Builds your skill

  • Try first, then ask AI to review your attempt
  • Ask for hints and explanations, not answers
  • Keep asking “why?” until you can explain it
  • Rebuild it later without AI

Builds dependence

  • Pasting the assignment, submitting the answer
  • Copying code you haven’t read
  • Asking AI before thinking about the problem
  • Being unable to explain your own project

Rule of thumb: if AI disappeared tomorrow, could you still do this?

(~2.5 min) The same tool can make you stronger or weaker. It depends on how you use it. A healthy study routine: try the problem yourself for 20 to 30 minutes first. Then ask the AI something like: “Here is my attempt. Don’t give me the solution. Point out my mistake and give me a hint.” Use AI as a tutor: ask it to quiz you, explain a concept with an analogy, or generate practice problems. After you finish, close the chat and rebuild the solution from scratch. If you can’t, you haven’t learned it yet. The rule of thumb on the slide is a good honesty check.
