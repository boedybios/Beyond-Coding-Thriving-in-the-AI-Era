05 · New in the AI era

AI’s role

What did AI help with, and how did you verify it?

06

Results

What worked, what didn’t, and what you’d change.

Put this in your README: it lets anyone see how you think.

(~1.5 min) A simple template for every project README or portfolio page: Problem, Constraints, Options, Decision, AI’s role, Results. Point out number 5: being honest about how you used AI, and how you checked its work, shows maturity. It’s a strength, not something to hide. Suggest they apply this template to one existing project this week.
