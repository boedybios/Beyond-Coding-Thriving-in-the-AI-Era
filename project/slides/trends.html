Before chasing a new trend, ask:

  1. Does it solve a problem I actually have?
  2. What concept is it built on?
  3. Can I build something small with it this week?

Tools come and go. The concepts underneath them carry over to whatever comes next.

(~2 min) New tools appear every week; you can’t and shouldn’t chase them all. Tools sit on top of concepts. Web frameworks keep changing, but developers who understand HTTP, the DOM, and state management move between them easily. The same is true for AI tools: products will change, but skills like giving clear context, evaluating output, and testing carry over. Use the three questions as a filter before investing time in something new. A practical routine: pick one or two trusted sources, like a newsletter, a community, or the release notes of tools you use. Spend about 30 minutes a week, and try anything promising by building something small.
